Who is building this.

A pre-seed venture investor is, in the end, buying the team. The rest of the site is the argument for the category, the methodology, and the wedge. This page is the argument for the people. We do not believe in pages that read like LinkedIn pulled into a website template, and we do not believe in the artificial modesty that pretends the founders have not done the work they have done. We are going to say plainly who we are, what we have done, and why we are the right people to build this company.

How we got here.

The lab opened in January 2026. This is the road from there to the judgement model.

  1. January 2026

    The lab opens, as a service.

    Companies asked us to capture how their senior people decide before those people moved on, so that is where we began, and we did it by hand.

  2. March 2026

    Shadow sessions.

    We sat beside experts while they worked and asked why at every turn. Our first pilot was with Brainoscope, a clinical neurotechnology firm: 54 sessions and 722 moments of judgement captured. It worked, and it could not scale, because every hour we captured cost an hour of ours.

  3. then

    A paper changes the plan.

    We came across research showing that a language model could do that probing itself. The expert just works at their screen, moving the cursor and pressing Enter, and the model asks what we used to ask.

  4. July to September 2026

    Underwriting.

    We took it to cyber underwriting desks in the London market, where senior underwriters told us it was a problem that needed solving. It was a great run, and it showed us what stood in the way.

  5. then

    Too slow, too costly.

    A language model was too slow and too costly to sit on every decision a desk makes. We wanted a model of our own for judgement, and we did not yet know enough statistics to build one.

  6. then

    Back to the lecture hall.

    The two of us worked through the Harvard and Stanford statistics lectures that anyone can watch on YouTube, a great many of them, until we could model the data ourselves.

  7. November 2026

    The judgement model works.

    It is ours, and it is cheap and fast to run. On Microsoft’s public GUIDE data it closes 84% of benign positives, at a setting where 1 in 100 true positives is closed as well.

  8. today

    CyberSOC.

    The first product built on it captures how a SOC’s analysts reason. See CyberSOC

We are hiring senior, slowly, and with the same restraint that runs through the rest of the company. We are not building a thirty-person organisation in year one. We do not believe a company at this stage of category creation benefits from the layer of operational friction that adding the next twenty people produces.
