Research.

Tacit Labs is a research lab first. Three papers are in work, one here and two at Birchlogic, the founders’ security practice. None of them is published yet, so each entry says plainly where it stands.

Papers in work.

  1. Tacit Labsworking title

    Can you tell a good call from a habit?

    Every desk that decides things for a living has a way of deciding, and every person on it has habits of their own. One analyst escalates anything that arrives over the VPN and another never does, and from the outside both look like judgement. This paper is about capturing how a team reasons from the decisions it has already made, and then separating what the team as a whole would stand behind from what is one person’s habit or bias.

    It matters because a system that learns from experts learns their habits too, unless something tells the two apart.

    Where it stands: in preparation. In a first test on synthetic decisions it found all seven patterns we had planted, with one false alarm. CyberSOC is where this work is first put to use.

  2. Birchlogicpaper in preparation

    Does safety survive compression?

    Every regulated deployment quantises its models to fit real hardware, and almost nobody has measured what that does to the safety behaviours the full-precision model was aligned with. The team extracted a refusal direction once at full precision and applied it unchanged across the compressed versions. The finding so far is uncomfortable in an interesting way: the geometry drifts, and the behaviour transfers anyway.

    Where it stands: in preparation, and independently reverified inside the lab before anything ships.

  3. Birchlogicre-running

    What does an on-premises security model actually cost?

    Running a security model inside your own walls is sold hard and measured badly, including, at first, by us. Our own first measurement pass failed our own review, so the work is being run again, and we would rather publish late and right than early and wrong.

    Where it stands: re-running. No numbers until it clears.
